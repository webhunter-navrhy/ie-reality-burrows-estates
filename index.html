Burrows Estates are delighted to present this very spacious, bright dual aspect apartment overlooking Dublin City. This two bedroom apartment offers very spacious living accommodation with the added convenience of an allocated parking space. Overlooking Dublin city, it is conveniently located very close to shops, restaurants, creches, M50, N11 and well serviced by bus routes to the city and Luas. Accommodation comprises entrance hall, a bright and large living room with corner balcony, fully fitted kitchen and dining area, master bedroom with ensuite bathroom and fitted wardrobes, second double bedroom with fitted wardrobes, main bathroom. Storage room and additional hall storage. Wooden floors throughout. GFCH. Allocated parking space. Excellent investment opportunity or suit owner occupiers.
